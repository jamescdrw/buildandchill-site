## What our air conditioning installation includes

Every install is done start to finish by our own team, based in Birstall. A typical job covers:

- A survey of the room and the outside wall to choose the right size of unit and the best route for the pipework
- Supplying the indoor and outdoor units from your chosen brand
- Mounting the indoor unit and fixing the outdoor unit on a wall bracket or ground stand
- Drilling through the wall and running the refrigerant pipes, cable and condensate drain

- Pressure testing, vacuuming and commissioning the system

Electrical and air conditioning work is carried out and certified by NAPIT and REFCOM F-Gas registered engineers, and our installers are City & Guilds trained.

## Choosing the right system

### Single split units

One indoor unit connected to one outdoor unit. This suits a single bedroom, home office, living room, garden room or small shop.

### Multi-split systems

Several indoor units run from one outdoor unit. It is a tidy option if you want to cool or heat two or more rooms without filling the outside of your house with outdoor units.

### Heating and cooling

Modern split systems work as air-to-air heat pumps, so they can heat a room in winter as well as cool it in summer.

## What affects the price of air con installation

Our prices start from £1,495, and the total depends on a few things:

- **How many rooms** you want covered, and whether that means several single splits or a multi-split
- **The pipe run** between the indoor and outdoor units. Longer runs mean more pipe, insulation and time
- **Back-to-back or not.** Where the outdoor unit sits directly behind the indoor unit on the other side of the wall, the job is simpler and usually the cheapest option
- **Wall type.** Solid brick, stone and concrete take more drilling than a cavity or timber-framed wall
- **The brand and size of unit** you choose
- **Electrics.** Some homes need a new circuit or work at the consumer unit
