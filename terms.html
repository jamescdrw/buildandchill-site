1. Registrations and who carries out regulated work

Some work we offer is regulated and must be done or certified by a registered business. That work is carried out under the following registrations, which are held by JBP Electrical, not by Build & Chill Property Services:

  • Electrical work, including notifiable work under Part P of the Building Regulations, is carried out under JBP Electrical's NAPIT registration. Electrical Installation Certificates, Minor Works Certificates and Building Regulations compliance certificates are issued by JBP Electrical.
  • Air conditioning installation, servicing and any work involving refrigerant gases is carried out under JBP Electrical's REFCOM F-Gas registration, in line with the F-Gas Regulations. Commissioning records and F-Gas paperwork are issued by JBP Electrical.

Where your job includes this work, your quote will say so. Registration numbers are available on request and will be shown on the certificates you receive. Our installers are City & Guilds trained.
