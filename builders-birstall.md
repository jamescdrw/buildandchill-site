Looking for a builder in Birstall? Build & Chill Property Services is based right here in Birstall, on the north side of Leicester. Ashley Finlay-Thurman and the team have been in the trade for 20 years, and we take on jobs of every size, from a dripping tap or a damaged ceiling to a full kitchen, bathroom or extension.

Because we are local, it is easy for us to pop round, measure up and give you a proper written quote.
