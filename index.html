Extensions, with the hard part done for you

Single and double storey extensions, kitchen-diners, wraparounds and garage conversions. Most people put off extending because of the paperwork, not the building. We handle all of it, so you deal with one person from first sketch to final sign-off.

  1. Drawings

    Measured survey and architectural drawings of your home and the new space.

  2. Planning

    We check whether you need planning permission or can build under permitted development, and submit the application to the council for you.

  3. Building control

    Building Regulations approval, inspections at every stage, and the completion certificate at the end.

  4. The build

    Foundations, brickwork, roof, windows, plastering, electrics and plumbing, then your new kitchen or bathroom fitted to finish.

How a job runs

One team for all the trades: building, plumbing, electrics, plastering, tiling and decorating. One point of contact from the first visit to the last.

  1. Survey

    We visit, measure up and listen to what you want from the room.

  2. Design and price

    A clear written quote. If it's a Howdens kitchen, we help you finalise the plan with your depot.

  3. Strip-out

    Old units, suites and tiles removed and taken away. Floors and furniture protected.

  4. First fix

    Plumbing, electrics, walls and floors put right before anything new goes in.

  5. Fit

    Units, worktops, sanitaryware, tiling and appliances installed and connected.

  6. Finish and handover

    Sealant, skirting, decorating and a proper clean. We walk you round it before we leave.
