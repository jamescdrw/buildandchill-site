## What our bathroom installation includes

A typical supply and fit covers:

- A survey to check pipework, drainage, ventilation, walls and floors
- Choosing your suite, furniture and finishes
- Stripping out the old bathroom and taking it away
- First fix plumbing, including moving pipes and wastes where needed
- Electrical work for lighting, extractor fans, shaver points or electric showers
- Plastering, boarding and waterproofing wet areas
- Fitting the bath or shower, toilet, basin and furniture
- Wall and floor tiling
- Decorating to finish

## How a bathroom installation runs

### Survey and planning

We look at what is there now, what you want to change and anything hidden we need to plan for, such as the route for wastes or the state of the floor.

### Strip out and first fix

The old suite and tiles come out. Pipes, wastes and cables are moved to suit the new layout.

### Boarding, plastering and waterproofing

Walls are made good or boarded, and showers and wet areas are properly waterproofed before tiling. Getting this stage right matters more than anything you can see at the end.

### Fitting and finishing

The suite and furniture go in, followed by tiling, second fix plumbing and electrics, sealing and decorating.

## What affects the price of a bathroom

Every bathroom is priced to the job. The main factors are:

- The size of the room
- The suite, furniture and tiles you choose
- Whether things are staying in the same place or moving
- Converting a bath to a walk-in shower or wet room
- The condition of the walls and floor once stripped
- Electrical work such as new lighting, fans, an electric shower or underfloor heating
