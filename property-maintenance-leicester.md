Build & Chill Property Services provides property maintenance in Leicester and across Leicestershire for homeowners, landlords and businesses. From building repairs to plastering, plumbing, electrics and decorating, our one team can take care of the jobs that keep a property in good shape.

We are builders based in Birstall, with 20 years in the trade.

## What our property maintenance covers

- General building repairs, brickwork and making good
- Plastering and patching walls and ceilings
- Plumbing repairs and replacing taps, toilets, sinks and radiators
- Electrical work, from extra sockets and lighting to new circuits
- Tiling repairs and re-grouting
- Internal doors, skirting, architraves and other joinery
- Decorating
- Repairs after leaks or water damage
- Work between tenancies for landlords

Electrical work is carried out and certified by NAPIT registered engineers.

## Building repairs in Leicester for homeowners

Small problems tend to grow if they are left. Cracked plaster, a dripping tap, a sticking door or a damp patch is easier to deal with sooner rather than later. Because our team covers several trades, we can often fix the cause and finish the job, for example repairing a leak, replacing damaged plaster and redecorating, without you needing to book three firms.

## Landlord maintenance

Landlords need repairs done properly and the property ready for the next tenant. We can help with:

- Repairs reported during a tenancy
- Refreshing a property between tenants, including decorating and repairs
- Updating tired kitchens and bathrooms
- Electrical work certified by NAPIT registered engineers

Landlords have legal duties on safety and repairs. Your local council's private housing team, such as Leicester City Council's, can advise on your obligations.

## What affects the price

Maintenance jobs are priced to the work. The main things that affect cost are:

- The size of the job and how long it will take
- Materials and any parts needed
- Access, such as working at height
- Whether hidden problems turn up, like rotten timber behind a leak
- Grouping several jobs into one visit, which is often better value

## Why one team for all the trades helps

Most repairs involve more than one trade. A leak can mean plumbing, plastering and decorating. One team means one visit to arrange, one point of contact and the job finished properly.
