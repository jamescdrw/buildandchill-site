## What our kitchen installation includes

A full supply and fit usually covers:

- A survey to measure up and check plumbing, electrics, walls and floors
- Design and ordering through Howdens
- Stripping out and disposing of the old kitchen
- First fix plumbing and electrics, including moving pipes, sockets or lighting
- Plastering and making good walls and ceilings
- Fitting cabinets, doors, drawers and panels
- Worktops, sink, taps and hob
- Fitting appliances
- Wall tiling or splashbacks
- Decorating to finish

We can also fit [utility rooms](/#kitchens) at the same time, which often makes sense while the trades are on site.

## How the job runs

### Survey and design

We visit, measure up and talk through what you want. Then you finalise the design and choices with Howdens.

### Strip out and first fix

The old kitchen comes out, and pipes and cables go where the new layout needs them. Any walls coming out or openings being made happen at this stage.

### Plastering and fitting

Walls are made good, then the cabinets go in, followed by worktops, sink and appliances.

### Finishing

Tiling, second fix electrics and plumbing, then decorating. We clean up as we go and leave you with a kitchen ready to use.

## What affects the price of a kitchen

Every kitchen is priced individually. The main things that affect the cost are:

- The size of the room and the number of units
- Your choice of doors, worktops and appliances
- Whether the layout is staying the same or changing
- How much plumbing and electrical work is needed
- The condition of walls, ceilings and floors once the old kitchen is out
- Extras such as knocking through, new flooring or a utility room

## Why one team for all the trades helps

Kitchens are where trades overlap the most. When the plumber, electrician, plasterer and fitter are all separate, you end up chasing people and waiting for gaps in their diaries. With one team, the work runs in the right order and you have one person to talk to.
