## What our suspended ceiling service covers

- Fitting LED panels and lighting into the grid
- Changes to existing ceilings for new layouts or partitions
- Making good around services such as vents, sprinklers and access hatches

## Office ceiling tiles and grid systems

Most commercial suspended ceilings use a metal grid with lay-in tiles, commonly in 600 x 600mm or 1200 x 600mm sizes. There is a wide choice of tiles, from basic mineral fibre to acoustic, moisture-resistant and easy-clean types for kitchens or washrooms.

The right choice depends on the space. A busy open-plan office may benefit from acoustic tiles, while a food preparation area needs a tile that can be cleaned. We will talk you through the options.

## How a commercial ceiling job runs

### Survey

We measure the area, check the height, look at what is above the ceiling and note lights, vents, sprinklers or detectors that need to fit into the new grid.

### Planning the work

We agree the specification, a programme and working times that suit your business.

### Installation

The perimeter trim and main grid go up first, levelled across the whole area. Then lights and services are set into the grid, and the tiles go in last.

### Finishing

We clean up, take away waste and leave the space ready to use.

## What affects the price of a suspended ceiling

Every ceiling is priced to the job. The main factors are:

- The floor area and ceiling height
- Whether it is a new installation, a replacement or tile swaps
- The type of grid and tiles, such as acoustic or moisture-resistant
- How many lights, vents and other services need to be built in
- Access, including whether scaffold towers or out-of-hours working are needed
- Removing and disposing of an old ceiling
